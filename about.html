About North Star Bakery

Our Story

Founded in 2018, North Star Bakery began with a simple wooden peel, a sourdough starter nurtured for over a decade, and a dream to bring neighbors together over honest, hand-shaped bread.

Sourcing Philosophy

We partner directly with regional family farms that practice sustainable agriculture. Our organic grains are stone-milled locally to preserve vital nutrients and deep, authentic flavor in every bite.

Staff Highlights

Elena Vance (Founder & Head Baker): Elena spent years training in European artisan mills before bringing her passion for wild-yeast fermentation home to our neighborhood.

Marcus Chen (Pastry Chef): A master of laminated doughs, Marcus ensures our morning pastry cases are filled with golden, flaky perfection every single day.
